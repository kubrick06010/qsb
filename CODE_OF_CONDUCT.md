# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want QSB to be a welcoming project for people who use, teach, study, test,
and improve operations research tooling.

Participants are expected to act with respect, patience, and generosity toward
others, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of expertise and different use cases.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them when possible.
- Help keep discussions on topic and useful for the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Public or private intimidation.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, documentation, and other public project communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
limit participation when behavior harms the project community.

If you see behavior that violates this code of conduct, report it to the
project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports with care
and avoid exposing private details unnecessarily.
